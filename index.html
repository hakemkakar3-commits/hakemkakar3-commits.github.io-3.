Biography in English

Miah Abdul Hakim Akhundzada Kakar, son of Abdul Halim, is originally from Zamin Oghor village in Mir Bacha Kot District, Kabul Province, Afghanistan. He was born on 10 Jadi 1363 (Solar Hijri calendar).

He studied Law and Political Science from 2011 to 2015 and obtained a bachelor's degree.

Professional Experience

  • Teacher and staff member at Ghazi Amanullah Khan School, Khair Khana, 2008–2018 (Solar Hijri years 1387–1397).
  • Teacher and staff member at Shaista Hewad School, Khair Khana, 2018–2019 (Solar Hijri years 1397–1398).
  • Teacher and staff member at Omar Khayyam School, Sarai Shomali, 2020–2021 (Solar Hijri years 1399–1400).
  • Head of Identification (Peyzand), Police District 9, Kabul City, serving since 2021 (Solar Hijri year 1400), according to the information provided.

Languages: Dari, Pashto, and English.
