Miyah Abdul Hakim Akhondzada Kakar

This page presents the biography, education and professional history of Miyah Abdul Hakim Akhondzada Kakar.

Father's name: Abdul Halim

Date of birth: 31 December 1984 (10 Jadi 1363)

Place of origin: Zamin Awghur Village, Mir Bacha Kot District, Kabul Province, Afghanistan

Professional Experience in Education

Current Position

According to the information provided, serving in the Identification section of Police District 9 in Kabul City since 1400 Solar Hijri.
